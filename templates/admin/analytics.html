Funnels by visit

The unit is a visit (one sitting, with no gap longer than 30 minutes), not a person over a period. Two kinds of row, and they must not be confused:
step — you cannot get past it, so it is filled in from the deepest one reached: a beacon that never arrived does not tear the funnel apart, and "paid" can never exceed "created an order".
observation — "scrolled as far as the pricing". Counted as it is, with no filling-in: someone may have arrived on a direct link straight to the form, and crediting them with "saw the pricing" for the sake of a tidy decreasing table would be a lie. That is why the transition from observations to steps can exceed 100% — a finding, not an error: that many people reached the form without the landing.
"Paid" comes from the order, not from an event: payment is confirmed by a webhook in which there is no browser at all. One visit can appear in both doors. Who exactly did what — the Visits section.

Channels (by visit)

The channel is decided by the visit's own tags: ads — a click id (gclid/fbclid/msclkid) or an advertising utm_medium, organic — a referral from search, internal — a move within the site. This is the LAST touch: the purchase is brought by the click after which the person bought, not by the one through which they first heard of us.
